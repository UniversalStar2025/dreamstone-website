We are DreamStone.

Based in Shanghai, DreamStone develops games and original products while exploring VR, AR, and digital interaction. We bring game development, interaction design, and visual creativity together to turn imagination into experiences.

Our work in web games, mobile games, and VR applications began in 2010. Since then, our experience has grown across automotive, industrial, education, military, and entertainment projects. In 2016, we received Shanghai's High-Tech Achievement Transformation Project certification.

Using technology and thoughtful design to help our partners go beyond the limits of physical experiences.
